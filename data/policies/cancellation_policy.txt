COMPANY CANCELLATION POLICY
Last Updated: January 2026

1. OVERVIEW
This policy explains how customers can cancel orders placed with TechShop Inc. and what happens after a cancellation.

2. CANCELLATION WINDOW
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of purchase
- After 24 hours, orders that have not shipped can still be cancelled with a 10% processing fee
- Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled; please use the refund process instead

3. HOW TO CANCEL
Step 1: Email support@techshop.com with your order number and "Cancellation" in the subject line
Step 2: Our team confirms cancellation eligibility within 1 business day
Step 3: You receive a cancellation confirmation email

4. REFUNDS FOR CANCELLED ORDERS
- Cancellations within 24 hours: full refund to the original payment method within 3-5 business days
- Cancellations after 24 hours: refund minus the 10% processing fee within 5-7 business days

5. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATIONS
- Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from your account page
- Access continues until the end of the current billing period
- No partial refunds for unused time in a billing period

6. CONTACT INFORMATION
For cancellation inquiries:
Email: support@techshop.com
Phone: 1-800-TECH-SHOP
Business Hours: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST
